# Ridge Club — video teardown

**Source:** YouTube `@theridgeclub` — the channel's only upload:
[Ridge Club – Luxury Ski Locker Establishment at Park City Mountain Resort](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i5-RE4Kwwj0) (0:59).
`@RidgeClub` exists but has no videos. Watched 2026-10-04 with `/watch`
(Gemini engine). Observations below are Gemini's, not frame-by-frame review.

## What it is

A members' slope-side ski locker club: heated lockers with boot dryers and
keypad locks, a changing lounge, and a door that opens straight onto the snow
near the lifts. The title says Park City Mountain Resort; Gemini guessed the
Canyons Village base, which the video itself does not confirm.

## What the video sells

Convenience, shown rather than said: boots on in the lounge, warm gear from
locker #82, a few steps out, click in, ski off. The return trip is a keypad
door and skis back in the locker.

## What it leaves out

- No prices, offers or membership details.
- No voiceover, no call to action, no URL. Only on-screen text is the
  ◆◆ RIDGE CLUB logo (wall sign, door glass).
- Soundtrack: ambient loop with the line *"I want to see what you made of it."*

## Read

Looks luxury, says nothing. A viewer who wants in has no price and no next
step. The gap to fill, whether Ridge Club is a client or a competitor:

1. **A next step.** End card with URL / "book a tour" and the season dates.
2. **The number.** Even "memberships from $X/season" or "limited lockers
   remain" turns a mood piece into a lead.
3. **Short-form cuts.** The 0:06–0:27 boots-to-snow run is a ready-made
   15-second vertical; the drone proximity shot (0:28) is the hook.
4. **A voice.** One line of VO or captions ("Your boots are already warm.")
   gives the silent footage a claim.
