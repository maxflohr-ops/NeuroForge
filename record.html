year ninety · may · entry 90-023

damage, class .3300: a fence line at the county's east quarter was found opened from the inside. no prints were taken because no prints were found. the fence is in the file. the animal is not.

year ninety · june · entry 90-031

a porch light on delilah road burned all month with no house under it. see lot 005. the light is in the file.

year ninety · july · entry 90-036

the cardinal was counted at the county line. one. it is always one. see the bestiary, plate ii.

year ninety · august · entry 90-041

a canister of film was left on the counter overnight. no one was seen. it is marked vii. it has not been developed. see the succession.

year ninety · september · entry 90-042

a trap was found at the county line, new-set and unsprung. a blue canary sat over it and sang, and did not move off. the trap is in the file.

year ninety · september · entry 90-043 · latest

the trap was collected. nothing was caught in it, and nothing has been since. see the bestiary, plate iii. filed under: prayer.
