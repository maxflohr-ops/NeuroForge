notice · posted at the county courthouse

in the year nineteen hundred and thirty-five the government sent photographers into the counties, that the hard times might be set down in a record. in the year nineteen hundred and forty-three the offices were dissolved. this one was not.

for ninety years the field office has photographed one county. it files the damage done by the animal. it prints the places and not the people. it keeps what it is given. it does not restock, it does not explain, and it does not close the file.

what is not known is not in the file.
