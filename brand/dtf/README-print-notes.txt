PRINTER NOTES
- White underbase comes from the alpha channel automatically — files are
  ready as-is for any standard DTF RIP.
- The cardinal has two semi-transparent strokes (the perch line at 50%,
  the tail-brace at 80%). Most RIPs handle this fine; if yours flattens
  transparency badly, ask the shop to keep those lines as tints of
  #7A1D1D, not solid.
- Bone-colored art on dark garments NEEDS the white underbase at full
  strength or it goes muddy — bone is a warm off-white, not white.
- Gang sheet spacing: keep at least 0.5" between elements.
- Do not add outlines, glows, or drop shadows to anything.

HOUSE RULES (for anyone assembling the sheet)
- "parva puella · magna somnia" lives on the seal ONLY — never set it
  as standalone type.
- The animal itself is never depicted. The cardinal is the cardinal.
- Lowercase is intentional everywhere except the seal mottos.
